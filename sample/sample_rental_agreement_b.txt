RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (TENANT-FRIENDLY MODEL)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1st September 2025, by and between:

LANDLORD: Rajesh Kumar, residing at 12 Maple Street, Koramangala, Bangalore, Karnataka 560034 ("Landlord")

TENANT: _________________________ ("Tenant")

PROPERTY: Flat No. 104, Sunrise Heights, 4th Block, Koramangala, Bangalore, Karnataka 560034 ("Premises")

The Landlord agrees to lease the Premises to the Tenant subject to the following terms and conditions:


CLAUSE 1: LEASE TERM

The lease term shall commence on 1st October 2025 and shall continue for a period of eleven (11) months ("Lease Term"). Upon expiry, this Agreement may be renewed upon mutual written agreement of both parties with thirty (30) days prior notice.


CLAUSE 2: RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

The monthly rent for the Premises shall be Rs. 23,000 (Rupees Twenty-Three Thousand Only), payable on or before the 10th day of each calendar month. A grace period of five (5) days is granted. A nominal late fee of Rs. 100 per day shall apply only if rent remains unpaid after the 15th day of the month.


CLAUSE 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT

The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 46,000 (Rupees Forty-Six Thousand Only), equivalent to two (2) months' rent, upon execution of this Agreement. The Landlord shall inspect the property jointly with the Tenant upon move-out and refund the entire deposit, minus documented repair costs for non-wear-and-tear damages, within fifteen (15) banking days of vacating.


CLAUSE 4: RENT ESCALATION

In the event of a renewal after eleven (11) months, the monthly rent shall increase by a maximum of 5%, subject to mutual agreement in writing. No mid-term or arbitrary increases shall be permitted.


CLAUSE 5: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

The Landlord shall be fully responsible for all major and structural repairs, including plumbing lines, electrical wiring, sanitary fixtures, water leakage, seepage, and external painting. The Tenant shall only be responsible for routine day-to-day minor maintenance (such as replacing light bulbs or minor tap washers) up to Rs. 500 per incident. Any pre-existing defects documented at move-in remain the sole responsibility of the Landlord.


CLAUSE 6: USE OF PREMISES

The Premises shall be used for residential purposes. Quiet remote work, study, and standard home-office setups are explicitly permitted provided they do not generate commercial foot traffic or disturb neighbours.


CLAUSE 7: GUESTS AND VISITORS

The Tenant has the right to invite family members and personal guests to stay at the Premises for up to fourteen (14) consecutive days without requiring prior approval or registration with the Landlord.


CLAUSE 8: EARLY TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement prior to expiry by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Upon expiration of the 30-day notice period and handover of keys, the Tenant shall pay an early departure fee equivalent to one (1) month's rent, and the remaining security deposit shall be refunded in full after utility adjustments.


CLAUSE 9: RIGHT OF ENTRY AND PRIVACY

The Landlord or their authorized representative may enter the Premises for essential inspections or maintenance only after providing at least twenty-four (24) hours' prior written notice to the Tenant, during reasonable daytime hours (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM), and with the Tenant present, except in case of verified emergency.


CLAUSE 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be resolved amicably through mediation in Bangalore before initiating any formal proceedings. Both parties agree to bear their respective mediation costs.
