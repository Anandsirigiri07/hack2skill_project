RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1st September 2025, by and between:

LANDLORD: Priya Sharma, residing at 45 Residency Road, Bangalore, Karnataka 560025 ("Landlord")

TENANT: _________________________ ("Tenant")

PROPERTY: Flat No. 302, Green Valley Apartments, 12th Cross, Indiranagar, Bangalore, Karnataka 560038 ("Premises")

The Landlord agrees to lease the Premises to the Tenant subject to the following terms and conditions:


CLAUSE 1: LEASE TERM

The lease term shall commence on 1st October 2025 and shall continue for a period of eleven (11) months ("Lease Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement. Upon expiry of the Lease Term, this Agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent of both parties.


CLAUSE 2: RENT

The monthly rent for the Premises shall be Rs. 25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand Only), payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via bank transfer to the Landlord's designated account. A late fee of Rs. 500 per day shall be charged for any payment received after the due date, without any grace period.


CLAUSE 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT

The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only), equivalent to six (6) months' rent, upon execution of this Agreement. The Landlord shall retain the right to deduct any amounts owed by the Tenant, including but not limited to unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, cleaning costs, painting charges, and penalty charges, from the security deposit. The remaining balance, if any, shall be refunded within ninety (90) days of the Tenant vacating the Premises and returning all keys.


CLAUSE 4: RENT ESCALATION

The monthly rent shall be increased by 15% at the commencement of each renewal period. The Tenant agrees to this escalation as a condition of renewal. The Landlord additionally reserves the right to apply further increases based on prevailing market conditions at the Landlord's sole discretion, provided such increases are communicated in writing at least thirty (30) days prior to the renewal date.


CLAUSE 5: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

The Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance and repairs to the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical systems, appliances, fixtures, structural repairs, waterproofing, and pest control. The Tenant shall bear the full cost of any repairs, regardless of whether the damage was caused by the Tenant, resulted from normal wear and tear, or was pre-existing at the time of taking possession. The Tenant shall use only service providers approved by the Landlord for any repair work.


CLAUSE 6: USE OF PREMISES

The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes. The Tenant shall not operate any business, home office, or commercial activity of any nature from the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Remote work or work-from-home arrangements shall also require written approval.


CLAUSE 7: GUESTS AND OCCUPANTS

The Tenant shall not permit any guest to stay at the Premises for more than two (2) consecutive nights without obtaining the prior written approval of the Landlord. The Tenant must provide the Landlord with the names and identification details of all guests staying overnight. Any unauthorized overnight stay shall constitute a breach of this Agreement and may result in immediate termination at the Landlord's discretion.


CLAUSE 8: EARLY TERMINATION

Should the Tenant wish to terminate this Agreement before the expiry of the Lease Term, the Tenant shall provide ninety (90) days' written notice and shall pay an early termination penalty equivalent to three (3) months' rent, in addition to forfeiting the security deposit. The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days' written notice, without any penalty, compensation, or obligation to refund the security deposit to the Tenant.


CLAUSE 9: RIGHT OF ENTRY

The Landlord or the Landlord's designated agents, representatives, or contractors shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without prior notice to the Tenant, for the purposes of inspection, maintenance, repairs, showing the property to prospective tenants, buyers, or lenders, or for any other reason deemed necessary by the Landlord. The Tenant shall not refuse or obstruct such entry under any circumstances.


CLAUSE 10: ALTERATIONS AND MODIFICATIONS

The Tenant shall not make any alterations, modifications, additions, improvements, or decorative changes to the Premises, including but not limited to painting, drilling, installation of shelves or fixtures, hanging of heavy items, or any structural changes, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any approved alterations shall become the permanent property of the Landlord upon termination of this Agreement, and the Tenant shall not be entitled to any compensation or reimbursement for such alterations.


CLAUSE 11: PETS

No pets of any kind, including but not limited to dogs, cats, birds, fish, or reptiles, shall be kept on or brought to the Premises at any time. Any violation of this clause shall result in an immediate penalty of Rs. 10,000 per occurrence and shall constitute grounds for immediate termination of this Agreement.


CLAUSE 12: SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT

The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, transfer, or otherwise grant possession of this Agreement or the Premises, in whole or in part, to any third party, temporarily or permanently, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any unauthorized subletting or transfer shall be considered a material breach of this Agreement and shall entitle the Landlord to immediately terminate the Agreement and forfeit the security deposit.


CLAUSE 13: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

The Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Landlord from and against all claims, damages, losses, costs, liabilities, and expenses, including attorney fees and legal costs, arising from or in connection with the Tenant's use or occupation of the Premises, regardless of fault or negligence. The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss of or damage to the Tenant's personal property, including in cases of theft, fire, flood, water damage, electrical failure, or other events, whether or not caused by the Landlord's negligence or failure to maintain the Premises.


CLAUSE 14: MOVE-OUT CONDITIONS

Upon vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall ensure that the property is restored to its original condition at the time of move-in, subject to reasonable wear and tear as determined solely by the Landlord. The Tenant shall engage a professional cleaning service approved by the Landlord, repaint all walls in the original color at the Tenant's expense, and replace any items identified by the Landlord as worn or damaged. Failure to comply with any move-out requirement shall result in deductions from the security deposit as determined by the Landlord.


CLAUSE 15: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Bangalore, Karnataka. The Tenant agrees to bear all of the Landlord's legal costs, including attorney fees, court fees, and related expenses, in the event that any dispute or legal proceeding is resolved in the Landlord's favor.


CLAUSE 16: FORFEITURE OF DEPOSIT

In the event of any breach of this Agreement by the Tenant, including but not limited to late payment of rent beyond seven (7) days, unauthorized alterations, unauthorized guests, pet violations, noise complaints, or violation of any clause contained herein, the Landlord shall have the right to forfeit the entire security deposit as liquidated damages, in addition to pursuing any other remedies available under applicable law. The Landlord's determination of whether a breach has occurred shall be final and binding.


CLAUSE 17: NOISE AND CONDUCT

The Tenant shall maintain a peaceful and quiet environment at the Premises at all times. No music, television, gatherings, parties, or other noise-producing activities shall be permitted after 9:00 PM or before 7:00 AM. The Tenant shall comply with all rules and regulations of the apartment complex and shall not engage in any activity that may cause disturbance to neighbors. Violations may result in written warnings and, upon second occurrence, immediate termination of this Agreement.


CLAUSE 18: UTILITIES

The Tenant shall be responsible for the timely payment of all utility charges, including electricity, water, gas, internet, cable, and any other services consumed at the Premises. The Tenant shall transfer all utility accounts to their name within seven (7) days of taking possession. Failure to pay utility charges on time shall be considered a breach of this Agreement.


CLAUSE 19: INSURANCE

The Landlord shall not be required to maintain any insurance coverage for the Tenant's personal belongings, valuables, or possessions. The Tenant is advised to obtain comprehensive renter's insurance at the Tenant's own expense. The Landlord's building insurance does not cover any loss or damage to the Tenant's property under any circumstances.


CLAUSE 20: ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, representations, warranties, and agreements of any kind, whether written or oral. This Agreement may not be amended, modified, or supplemented except by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver by the Landlord of any breach shall be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach.
